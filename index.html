About PawSync

Smarter Pet Care Starts with a Better Routine

At PawSync, we understand that pet parents want to provide consistent care even when life gets busy.

Work, school, travel and changing schedules can make regular feeding times difficult to maintain. PawSync was created to make everyday feeding simpler, smarter and more dependable.

Our smart feeding solution combines convenient scheduling, portion management and connected technology to help pet parents manage their pets' routines with greater confidence.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: use practical technology to make everyday pet care easier while supporting healthier and more consistent routines.

PawSync is designed around three principles:

Convenience Pet care should fit into modern lifestyles.
Consistency Reliable routines contribute to better everyday care.
Connection Technology should help pet parents feel connected even when they cannot be physically present.

Because when their routine is taken care of, you can spend less time worrying—and more time enjoying life together.
